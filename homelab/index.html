Homelab

My homelab is where I experiment with networking, Linux, security monitoring, development, virtualization, and infrastructure. I use it to build systems, troubleshoot real problems, and test ideas outside of a classroom or preconfigured lab environment.

Services and Capabilities

  • VPN / Remote Access: remote administration with Tailscale.
  • Intrusion Detection: Suricata-based monitoring.
  • Traffic Monitoring: WatcherDash alert review and connection observations.
  • Managed Networking: managed switching and network configuration.
  • Linux Infrastructure: Linux administration and self-hosted services.
  • Virtualization: Proxmox and virtual-machine backup workflows.
  • Development Services: local website previews and software experiments.

Security Monitoring

The lab includes Suricata-based IDS monitoring and WatcherDash for reviewing alerts and selected connection activity. Coverage depends on the traffic the sensor receives; an alert is a starting point for investigation.

The Suricata and WatcherDash project documents the setup and its limitations. Quick Bits collects individual investigations and troubleshooting writeups.
